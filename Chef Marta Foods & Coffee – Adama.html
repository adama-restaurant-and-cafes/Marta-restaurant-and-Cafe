three ways to eat.

Ethiopian tables follow the calendar. On fasting days the food is fully plant-based; on other days meat and dairy return. Chef Marta's keeps both under one roof, so a family with different needs can still sit together.

And when the plates are cleared, the coffee shop takes over: macchiato, spris and the full coffee ceremony.

About us

Chef Marta

A family-friendly Adama table where fasting, non-fasting and coffee lovers all find their door.

Chef Marta

Cooked fresh,
shared together.

Chef Marta's is built around a simple idea: one family should be able to sit at one table, whatever they are eating today. A fasting grandmother, a hungry cousin and a coffee-loving friend each get a kitchen that understands them.

Our team prepares dishes fresh through the day, keeps the fasting kitchen fully plant-based, and finishes every meal with proper Ethiopian coffee.

Questions

How early should we order?

48 hours for groups under 30, one week for weddings and large events.

Can fasting and non-fasting guests share one order?

Yes. Choose the Mixed Table and we prepare both kitchens separately.

Do you deliver?

We deliver in Adama and nearby. Delivery fee depends on distance.

Can you serve coffee at our event?

Yes. Our Buna Ceremony Service brings the jebena, incense and an attendant.
